# Failure-first expectations

- A cited account/rate hint covers only part of the full treatment. Equal account and semantic rational rate remain unknown_exposure, never accepted_unchanged.
- A cited hint contradicted by any reviewed line's account or exact rate is corrected, even though deduction and other treatment dimensions were not suggested.
- Uncited exposure and foreign citation authority remain conservative; this change does not infer clean labels.
- A sealed export reports independent and corrected counts separately. Legacy sealed exports without new optional metadata decode unchanged.
- An offline report retains separate independent/corrected populations and explicitly marks corrected as selected disagreement cases, not representative accuracy evidence.
